## Phase boundary

The historical broad TEST_PLAN canonical scenario includes splits, receipts, merchant/tag dimensions, refunds, investment purchase/disposal/revaluation and executed FX. These remain Phase2+ product scope, NOT RUN here. Do not classify them PASS or implement them to satisfy S09. Phase1 retains approved INR posting, all S08 typed commands and the staged FX restriction. Loan proceeds outside existing openings are not a new S09 command. Reconciliation is the owner-approved audited comparison, never a balance overwrite or adjustment journal.
